BeWhim ✦ Field Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BeWhim is a travel-discovery app: you swipe curated places, save the ones you like, and turn them into a day plan — alone or with friends. This page explains exactly what data BeWhim handles and why. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to make the app work, we don't sell anything, and there are no ads or trackers.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored as a secure hash by our auth provider — we never see it), and the display name you choose.
  • Your activity in the app: spots and guides you save, check-ins ("Passport" stamps), and — in group rooms — the rooms you belong to and your votes on spots, which are visible to the other members of that room.
  • Things you publish: trips you publish, places you submit, your optional @handle, and who you follow. These are public to other BeWhim users, along with your display name.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report content or block someone, we keep that record so we can review it and hide their content from you.
  • Location (optional): only when you use "Near me" or check in. For "Near me", your coordinates are sent to our server to find places around you; we keep an anonymous ~1 km area result for an hour to speed up the next search, and it is not linked to you. Check-in proximity is checked on your device. You can decline the permission and use everything else.
  • Usage and crash data: which features you use (for example "viewed a route", "opened in Maps", "saved a guide") and error reports when the app crashes, with your app version. These are linked to your account so we can understand how BeWhim is used and fix bugs. They are never shared or sold.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push token for your device.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting your data. Ever.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs — usage data stays in our own database.
  • No reading your contacts, photos, or anything else the app doesn't need.

Where your data lives

BeWhim runs on Supabase (database, authentication, and serverless functions). Your session token is stored on your device in the iOS Keychain. Every database table is protected by row-level security: you can only read or write your own private data (or a room's data if you're a member of that room), and everyone can read only what has been published.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — account, database, and hosting (see above).
  • Mapbox — renders the day-plan map and geocodes place names. Map tiles are requested from Mapbox when you view a map.
  • Google Routes — provides public-transit directions between the stops of your plan. Only the coordinates of the stops are sent, never your identity.
  • Google Places — finds places for "Near me", search, and submitted places, and serves place photos. Only place names or coordinates are sent, never your identity.
  • Anthropic — writes short place descriptions and tips. Only place details are sent, never your identity.
  • Expo push service — delivers notifications if you allow them.
  • Pexels — the source of spot photography.

Deleting your account

Settings → Danger zone → Delete account, right in the app. This permanently deletes your account and everything attached to it — saved spots, check-ins, room memberships, votes, published trips and places, follows, and reports — immediately and irreversibly. Usage and crash records are kept without your account link, so they can no longer be connected to you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll note it in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
